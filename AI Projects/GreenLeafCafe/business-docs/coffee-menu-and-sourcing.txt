Green Leaf Cafe — Coffee Menu and Bean Sourcing

Our Coffee Menu

Green Leaf Cafe serves a full range of coffee drinks, from straightforward espresso-based classics to specialty seasonal favorites:

- Espresso — a concentrated shot, the base for most of our drinks.
- Americano — espresso lengthened with hot water for a lighter, black-coffee style.
- Cappuccino — equal parts espresso, steamed milk, and milk foam.
- Latte — espresso with a larger portion of steamed milk and a light layer of foam.
- Macchiato — espresso "marked" with a small amount of foamed milk.
- Flat White — espresso with steamed milk and a thin, velvety layer of microfoam.
- Cortado — espresso cut with a small, equal amount of warm milk.
- Affogato — a scoop of vanilla-style dessert topped with a shot of hot espresso.
- Iced Coffee — chilled brewed coffee served over ice.
- Cold Brew — coffee steeped in cold water for 12+ hours for a smooth, low-acid flavor.
- Nitro Cold Brew — cold brew infused with nitrogen for a creamy, cascading texture.
- Irish Coffee — hot coffee with a touch of sweetness, topped with cream.
- Vienna Coffee — strong coffee topped generously with whipped cream.
- Dalgona Coffee — whipped instant coffee served over milk, known for its light, foamy top.
- Golden Latte — a turmeric-spiced milk latte with a mild espresso kick.
- Matcha Latte — steamed milk with ceremonial-grade matcha, for customers who want a coffee-shop experience without the coffee.

Customer Favorites

Based on what our regulars order most often, our most popular drinks are the Cappuccino, the Latte, and the Cold Brew — customers consistently mention these as the ones they come back for. The Dalgona Coffee and Matcha Latte have also become popular with newer visitors looking for something a little different, especially during warmer months.

How We Source Our Coffee Beans

Green Leaf Cafe sources its coffee beans from small farms and cooperatives within roughly 50 miles of Springfield, working directly with local roasters rather than large-scale commercial suppliers. Beans are selected in small batches so we can keep close track of freshness and quality, and roasting happens frequently and in small quantities rather than in one large run — this keeps every batch tasting closer to how the roaster intended it, rather than losing flavor while sitting in storage.

Once beans arrive at the cafe, they're stored in sealed, airtight containers away from light and heat, and we grind beans to order for each drink rather than pre-grinding in bulk, since ground coffee loses its flavor far faster than whole beans. This attention to sourcing and freshness is part of why Green Leaf Cafe's coffee has built such a loyal following among regulars.
