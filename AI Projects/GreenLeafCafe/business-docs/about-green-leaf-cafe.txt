About Green Leaf Cafe

Green Leaf Cafe was founded in Springfield with a simple idea: a neighborhood coffee shop that treats its customers like regulars from day one, serves honest food made from ingredients grown nearby, and never compromises on the quality of its coffee.

Our Story

Green Leaf Cafe started as a small counter serving coffee to early risers on their way to work. Word spread quickly about the quality of the espresso and the warmth of the staff, and within a year the cafe expanded into the full sit-down space it is today at 123 Market Street. What hasn't changed is the philosophy: source responsibly, roast carefully, and make every visitor feel like they've found their new favorite spot.

How We Operate

Green Leaf Cafe is open seven days a week, from 8:00 AM to 8:00 PM, so customers can stop by for an early coffee, a midday lunch, or an evening treat. Orders can be placed at the counter for dine-in or takeaway, and customers can also call ahead to have an order ready for pickup. For larger groups of six or more, we recommend contacting us in advance so we can prepare a table and make sure everyone is served promptly.

Our menu covers three main areas: a full coffee bar, an all-day breakfast menu, and a dedicated vegan menu, so there's something for nearly every visitor regardless of dietary preference. Free Wi-Fi is available for anyone who wants to linger with a laptop or a book.

Our Values

- Community first: we aim to know our regulars by name and make new visitors feel just as welcome.
- Local sourcing: our coffee beans and fresh produce come from farms and roasters within 50 miles of Springfield whenever possible.
- Quality over speed: every drink is made to order, not batch-brewed and left to sit.
- Inclusivity: our vegan menu is clearly marked and taken as seriously as every other dish we serve.
